01 — About

A few things about me.

I'm an engineer with a Master's degree in Mechatronics from NUST, with 2+ years of experience developing AI-powered healthcare solutions. My work sits at the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and medical devices — specifically gait assessment and physical frailty detection using depth sensors and motion capture.

I've led the development of indigenous AI systems for international collaborations with Japanese (Juntendo University) and Korean research teams, including on-site deployment and researcher training. The systems I build run on real edge hardware — Raspberry Pi 5, Azure Kinect, NVIDIA Jetson — because hospital floors don't have GPUs.

I'm fluent in English with extensive experience in multicultural research teams. I write here about the work, what I'm reading, and the things I'm still figuring out.

03 — Projects

Things I've built.

International Collaboration · Japan · 2025

AI-Powered Physical Frailty Assessment System

Built with Azure Kinect DK and Xbox Kinect for geriatric assessment. 3D skeletal tracking with 25-joint body model. CNN-LSTM architecture trained on 150+ elderly participants, validated against SPPB and Fried Frailty Criteria. Delivered and trained the team at Juntendo University, Tokyo.

Indigenous Gait Analysis System for Neurological Disorders

Embedded gait analysis on Raspberry Pi 5 with monocular camera. MediaPipe pose estimation + custom ResNet-50. Validated against Vicon (r = 0.91). Deployed at multiple clinical sites via Docker.
